What this plugin is meant to do is let an operator hand somebody an address that
lets that person set up their own account, rather than the operator creating an
account and then passing a password along by some other route.

Before it is any use the address has to be reachable by the person being invited,
and the operator has to have decided what a newly arrived account may see before
the invitation is sent rather than afterwards.

What it does with data about a person is take whatever a new account is set up
with, which is the account itself. It is meant to stay on the operator's host.
That is what the plugin is meant to do, and none of it is built yet.
