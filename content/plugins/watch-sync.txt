The plugin is meant to keep what a person has watched consistent across two
paired servers, so that a title finished on one is not offered again as unwatched
by the other. It is for somebody who has an account on two servers rather than
for two people, and that distinction is what decides who the data is about.

Before it is any use a pair has to exist, which is what the server-pairing plugin
is for, and the same person has to be identifiable on both sides. Two accounts
that happen to share a name are not the same person, and deciding that they are
would be the plugin quietly merging two viewing histories.

What it does with data about a person is move their watch history from one
server to the other, which is data crossing a boundary between two machines that
may have different operators. That is exactly the case a per-plugin data line
exists for, and it is the intended behaviour rather than a description of code
that runs today.
