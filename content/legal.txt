Who publishes this site

description: Who publishes this site, how to reach them, and which of those
  answers has not been taken yet.

A reader who wants to know who is behind a project should not have to read a
commit history to find out. This page is where that answer lives, and it is one
page rather than a paragraph on another, because somebody looking for it looks
for a page with this name.

Every answer below is read out of the tree rather than written into the page, so
an answer that changes is a change to one value and not an edit to prose. Where
an answer has not been taken, the line says so and says what it is waiting on,
rather than leaving an empty space that reads as an answer of nothing.

Publishing a site under a name of one's own carries a provider identification
duty in some jurisdictions. Which one applies turns on where the publisher sits
and on whether a site is read as private or as commercial, and none of that is
decided by anything in the tree that produces these pages. Nothing here is legal
advice.

What happens to a request for a page on this site is a separate question, and it
has its own page rather than a summary here. A summary would be a second copy of
those statements, and a second copy is what goes quietly out of step with the
first.
